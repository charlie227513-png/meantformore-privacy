Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Meant for More ("the app", "we", "us") is a digital-wellbeing app that helps you block distracting apps and earn breaks. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what we do not do with it.

What we collect

  • Photos you take for outdoor verification. When you tap "Go touch some grass" and take a photo, that image is sent to Google's Gemini AI (via our secure proxy server) to check whether you are outdoors. The photo is used solely for this check and is not stored by us after the check is complete. Google's AI processing is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • App-usage data you create yourself. Your focus blocks (app names, schedules), flashcard decks, and streak count are stored locally on your device using Android's Room database. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any server.
  • No account required. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal identifier.

What we do not collect

  • We do not read the content of your messages, emails, or other apps.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party for advertising or analytics purposes.
  • We do not use any third-party analytics SDKs (e.g. Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel).

Accessibility Service

The app uses Android's Accessibility Service solely to detect which app is in the foreground — this is required to show the block screen the moment a blocked app opens. The service does not read screen content, capture text, or record any user interaction.

Permissions explained

  • Accessibility — detects foreground app for blocking.
  • Display over other apps — shows the full-screen block overlay.
  • Camera — takes the outdoor-verification photo (used only when you initiate it).
  • Notifications — shows a persistent notification to keep the blocking service running in the background.
  • Battery optimisation exemption — prevents Android from killing the blocking service overnight.

Data retention

All data (blocks, cards, streaks) is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. We have no server-side copy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
